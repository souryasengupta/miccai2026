How this was built, and what it can and cannot tell you

Where the data comes from. The paper list is parsed from the official MICCAI 2026 Oral, Spotlight and Poster Schedule (revised 8 September 2026): 1,166 papers, with board numbers running without a gap across all five poster sessions. Titles and the MICCAI page and PDF links are taken from the MICCAI 2026 Open Access index, via a public community mirror (github.com/kaiwenli325/MICCAI-2026-paper-summary), because the build environment for this page could not reach papers.miccai.org directly. The links follow the official URL pattern but were not individually checked from here. One schedule paper, T-AM-098, has no Open Access entry and carries schedule data only.

How the labels were made. Every label is produced by keyword rules applied to the paper's title and, where one exists, its public arXiv abstract (412 papers). No subject categories from MICCAI or from the mirror are used. Papers marked abstract verified were classified from a real abstract; papers marked title only from the title alone, which is systematically thinner. A label needs either a title match or, for generic words, two independent mentions in the abstract, which suppresses passing references. Where nothing could be established the cell says Not stated or Unspecified rather than carrying a guess.

What is still missing. 516 papers have no organ, 556 no modality, 158 no task, 328 no method, and 845 no dimension. Most of these are titles that simply never name the anatomy, the scanner or the method, for papers with no public abstract. They are gaps in the evidence, not errors.

Ambiguous words. Most errors found while building this came from words with two meanings in this literature, and each was narrowed once found: diffusion (a model, or an MRI acquisition); enhancing (improving a method, or enhancing an image); volumetric (3D, or volumetry); lesion (cancer, or pneumonia and MS); saliency; calibration; angiography; bias; stitch; expert (a human, or mixture-of-experts); causal attention (transformer masking, not causal inference). Several were substrings inside other words — "covariance" contains "ovarian", "neurological" contains "urolog", "DBT" is also a model name. Expect residual errors of the same kind, and please report any you find.

Oral and spotlight flags. Read from the nine programme pages by detecting each page's column boundaries and splitting every column at its "Oral Presentations" and "Spotlight Presentations" headers. The structure validates — 18 oral blocks and 9 spotlight blocks, exactly sessions O1A–O6C — and yields 84 orals and 52 spotlights against an expected 90 and 45, so Poster only means "not detected as oral or spotlight". A paper's oral session and its poster session are unrelated.

Multimodal is kept apart from vision-language. Most titles that say "multimodal" mean multi-sequence MRI or imaging fused with genomics or clinical variables, not language, so they carry multimodal fusion. Vision-language covers image-text work, VQA, medical question answering and retrieval-augmented generation.
